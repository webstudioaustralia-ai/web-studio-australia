# Web Studio Australia - Brand Guidelines

## 🎨 Brand Identity

### Brand Name
**Web Studio Australia** - Premium Web Design & Development Studio

### Brand Tagline
"Crafting Digital Excellence for Australian Businesses"

### Brand Mission
To empower Australian businesses with innovative web solutions that drive growth, engagement, and success in the digital landscape.

### Brand Values
- **Innovation**: Cutting-edge technology and creative solutions
- **Excellence**: Highest quality standards in every project
- **Collaboration**: Working closely with clients as true partners
- **Reliability**: Consistent delivery and trustworthy service
- **Local Focus**: Deep understanding of Australian market needs

---

## 🎯 Brand Colors

### Primary Colors
- **Deep Blue**: `#003D82` - Trust, professionalism, reliability
- **Ocean Blue**: `#0066CC` - Energy, innovation, growth
- **Gold Accent**: `#FFB81C` - Premium, excellence, quality

### Secondary Colors
- **Light Gray**: `#F5F5F5` - Clean backgrounds
- **Dark Gray**: `#333333` - Text and contrast
- **Success Green**: `#00C853` - Positive actions
- **Alert Red**: `#E53935` - Warnings and errors

### Color Usage
```
Primary CTA Button: Ocean Blue (#0066CC)
Secondary CTA Button: Deep Blue (#003D82)
Accents & Highlights: Gold (#FFB81C)
Background: Light Gray (#F5F5F5)
Text: Dark Gray (#333333)
```

---

## 🔤 Typography

### Headings
- **Font**: Poppins Bold
- **Sizes**: H1: 48px, H2: 36px, H3: 28px, H4: 24px
- **Color**: Deep Blue (#003D82)
- **Line Height**: 1.2

### Body Text
- **Font**: Inter Regular
- **Size**: 16px
- **Color**: Dark Gray (#333333)
- **Line Height**: 1.6

### Accent Text
- **Font**: Poppins SemiBold
- **Size**: 14px
- **Color**: Ocean Blue (#0066CC)
- **Use**: Feature highlights, special offers

---

## 📐 Logo Usage

### Logo Variations
1. **Full Logo**: Logo + Text (Primary)
2. **Logo Mark Only**: Icon only (Secondary)
3. **Horizontal Layout**: For headers and footers
4. **Vertical Layout**: For sidebars and compact spaces

### Minimum Size
- Print: 40mm width
- Digital: 200px width
- Mobile: 120px width

### Clear Space
- Minimum 20px clear space around logo
- No logo overlap with other elements

### Background Usage
- Light backgrounds: Use full-color logo
- Dark backgrounds: Use white logo
- Never place on competing patterns

---

## 🖼️ Imagery Style

### Photography
- **Style**: Modern, professional, Australian-focused
- **Tone**: Bright, optimistic, inclusive
- **People**: Diverse, real, authentic
- **Locations**: Australian landscapes, local offices

### Illustrations
- **Style**: Minimalist, geometric, modern
- **Color**: Use brand color palette
- **Purpose**: Conceptual explanations, feature highlights

### Icons
- **Style**: Consistent stroke weight (2px)
- **Size**: 24px, 32px, 48px
- **Color**: Ocean Blue or Dark Gray

---

## ✏️ Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice
- **Professional yet Approachable**: Expert knowledge delivered conversationally
- **Confident**: We know our craft
- **Helpful**: Always solution-focused
- **Authentic**: Genuine Australian perspective

### Communication Guidelines
- Use "we" and "our" to build connection
- Be specific and avoid jargon
- Show results and real examples
- Celebrate client success
- Maintain optimistic tone

### Writing Examples

❌ **Avoid**: "Utilize our comprehensive digital transformation solutions to optimize your online presence metrics."

✅ **Use**: "We'll help your business grow online with web design and development that actually works."

## 🎬 Brand Applications

### Website
- Header: Full logo + navigation
- Color scheme: Primary blues with gold accents
- Imagery: Professional photography and minimalist illustrations
- CTA buttons: Ocean Blue backgrounds

### Business Cards
- Size: 90mm x 55mm
- Front: Logo, name, title
- Back: Contact information, social media
- Material: Premium cardstock with optional gold foil

### Email Templates
- Header: Brand colors and logo
- Body: Clean typography with consistent spacing
- CTA: Prominent Ocean Blue buttons
- Footer: Social links and contact info

### Social Media
- Cover images: Hero shots with brand colors overlay
- Profile pictures: Logo mark only
- Post templates: Consistent color blocks and typography
- Hashtags: #WebStudioAustralia, #DigitalExcellence

### Presentations
- Title slides: Deep Blue background with gold accents
- Body slides: Light Gray background with brand colors
- Data visualization: Ocean Blue and complementary colors
- Font: Poppins for headings, Inter for body

---

## 🚫 Brand Don'ts

- ❌ Don't modify the logo or change its proportions
- ❌ Don't use unofficial color variations
- ❌ Don't place logo on busy backgrounds
- ❌ Don't use outdated fonts or typography
- ❌ Don't modify the brand voice or tone inconsistently
- ❌ Don't use low-quality imagery
- ❌ Don't add shadows or special effects to the logo
